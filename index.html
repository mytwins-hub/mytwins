FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Everything you want to know
Does MY TWINS use facial recognition?
No. MY TWINS does not automatically analyse or process photos. Images are voluntarily uploaded by users and visible only to other registered members. The comparison is visual and subjective — done by the user themselves, exactly like any social network.
Is my personal data safe?
Yes. Your name and email are never visible to other users. You are identified by an anonymous number. Data is not sold to third parties. You can delete your account and all your data at any time from settings.
Does the photo have to be real?
MY TWINS does not verify the authenticity of photos — exactly like any other social network. It is the user's responsibility to upload truthful images. The matching system is based on self-declared physical characteristics, not on image analysis.
How does the anonymous chat work?
MY TWINS internal chat allows two users to communicate using only their anonymous nicknames. Neither can see the other's email or personal data. If you decide to exchange contacts, you do so freely within the chat — MY TWINS is not involved and bears no responsibility for that communication.
What if someone harasses me in chat?
You can block and report any user at any time. MY TWINS will moderate reports and may suspend or ban accounts that violate usage rules. Community safety is a priority.
Can I delete my account?
Yes, at any time with no waiting. Go to profile settings, click "Delete account" and all your data — photos, characteristics, messages — will be permanently deleted within 30 days.
Do the refinement sliders change my profile?
No. The refinement sliders are only used to refine your search for the ideal twin — they do not modify your public profile or the characteristics others see on you in any way.
